### Added

- `autospec_core::process_evidence`: process-evidence invariants (issue #4145). A conversion pass was declared dead twice on evidence that could not have shown it either way — it had in fact run for 88 minutes and converted 8 patches. The module makes the three invariants checkable: (1) a redirect you added is not evidence about output you did not route there — `capture_routed` confirms the reader's source is the place the program actually writes, and `unrouted_capture_finding` fires when a wrapper reads a stream the program never writes to (a program with its own `LOG=` destination leaves a stdout capture empty on every run, success and failure alike — useless precisely when it looks most damning); (2) a process-liveness check must not be able to match itself — `LivenessCheck` distinguishes a PID recorded at launch and verified with `kill -0` (a specific identity the checker can never be) from a `pgrep -f` pattern (which scans full command lines, including the command line of the shell invoking it), and `liveness_verdict` returns `SelfReferential` for a pattern check whose only matches are its own caller (`self_referential_finding`); (3) "produced no output" and "is not running" are two claims needing two pieces of evidence — `diagnose` combines the output evidence and the liveness evidence and names the claim(s) a "dead and produced nothing" conclusion is not allowed to rest on (`diagnosis_finding`). Regression tests (`crates/autospec-core/tests/process_evidence.rs`) reconstruct the incident end-to-end: the empty stdout capture read as "died", the `pgrep -f 'convpass.sh 3194'` that returned the two PIDs of its own caller, and the terminal line `converted=8 held=4 skipped=1` the reader missed in the program's own log.
